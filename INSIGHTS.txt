INSIGHTS


Sales over Months

Sales showed fluctuations throughout the period, with noticeable peaks in early 2016 and early 2017.
The highest monthly sales were recorded around March 2016, exceeding 40K.
Overall sales remained relatively stable with periodic spikes indicating seasonal demand.

Sales by Region

The West region generated the highest sales, contributing nearly 280K in revenue.
The East region ranked second in sales performance, with approximately 250K in sales.
The South region recorded the lowest sales among all regions, contributing around 150K.

Sales by Category

Technology was the top-performing category, accounting for approximately 38% of total sales.
Furniture contributed about 33% of total sales, making it the second-largest category.
Office Supplies represented nearly 29% of sales, the smallest share among the three categories.
